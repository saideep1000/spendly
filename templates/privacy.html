Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, so that we can display and summarize your spending.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We may also use your email address to send important account or service-related notices. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party services for infrastructure needs such as hosting and email delivery, and those providers only process data as necessary to provide their services to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
